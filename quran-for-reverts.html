Syllabus and Learning Stages

This sequence is adapted to the assessed starting level; familiar material can be reviewed and difficult skills revisited.

1

Agree on immediate priorities

Discuss reading, prayer passages and the questions you want to address first.

2

Begin Arabic reading

Practise letters, short vowels and blending through clear demonstrations and suitable examples.

3

Study short prayer passages

Learn accurate reading of suitable passages such as Al-Fatihah and short surahs, with explanations matched to your level.

4

Build a continuing routine

Revisit corrections, develop confidence reading new examples and choose further Quran, Arabic or Islamic studies goals.

What Happens in a Lesson

  1. Review a familiar sound, word or short passage.
  2. Ask questions about the material being studied.
  3. Hear a demonstration and practise aloud with patient correction.
  4. Connect the task with a suitable reading or prayer-learning goal.
  5. Agree on a small practice step you can maintain.

Assessment and Feedback

Progress is checked through the reading or learning task agreed with the teacher. You can revisit an idea without pressure to keep up with another learner; the next goal should reflect what is becoming clear and manageable.

Materials and Setup

  • A beginner Arabic reading text selected for your level.
  • A clear Mushaf and suitable translation when helpful.
  • A notebook for sounds, key terms and questions.
  • Teacher-selected introductory resources for any additional Islamic studies goal.

Confirm the required edition and any material costs with the team before purchasing resources.

Realistic Progression

There is no requirement to finish a reading course before learning other essentials or becoming Muslim. Reading, worship knowledge and understanding develop through different kinds of study. Discuss priorities and progress regularly rather than expecting to learn everything at once.

Possible next step: Introductory Islamic studies for everyday foundations. The teacher can help you choose when the foundations are ready.
