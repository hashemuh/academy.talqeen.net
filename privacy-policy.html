What We Collect

We collect your name, contact details (WhatsApp number and/or email), and information you share about the student (age, level, learning goals) — only what is needed to provide lessons.

How We Use It

Your information is used solely to match you with a teacher, schedule lessons, and communicate about your progress. We do not use your data for marketing without consent.

Who We Share It With

We share your name and relevant lesson information with your assigned teacher. We do not sell or share your data with any third parties.

For students under 18, we communicate with parents or guardians. We never collect personal data from children directly without parental consent.
