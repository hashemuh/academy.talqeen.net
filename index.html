About Talqeen Academy

Talqeen Academy is the live-teaching arm of Talqeen — the educational platform and technology behind the Talqeen Quran-reading app. While the app teaches letter-by-letter Quran reading at your own pace, the Academy offers live one-to-one lessons with qualified human teachers for those who want personal guidance, structured courses, and ongoing accountability.

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever takes a path upon which to obtain knowledge, Allah makes the path to Paradise easy for him."

Our teachers are native Arab speakers with expertise in Quranic sciences, Tajweed, Arabic language instruction, and Islamic Studies. We serve students of all ages and backgrounds — children, adults, converts, and families — across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and beyond.
