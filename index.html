Children & Families

Is your child learning Quran but not truly connecting with it?

We go beyond letters and words. Our teachers help children understand what they recite and build a genuine love for the Quran — the kind that shapes character and lasts a lifetime. Lessons are patient, child-friendly, and structured around your child's pace and attention span.

Arabic Language

Do you want to speak, read, or understand Arabic?

Master Arabic with qualified native-speaking tutors — Quranic Arabic, Modern Standard Arabic, or conversational Arabic. Our courses are tailored to your goal, your current level, and your schedule. Many students start by wanting to understand the Quran and end up reading classical texts.

Tajweed & Recitation

Do you want to recite the Quran beautifully and correctly?

Tajweed is not optional — the Quran was revealed to be recited with precision. Our Tajweed courses teach you makharij, sifaat, madd rules, and ghunnah through live correction in every lesson. You hear the right sound and immediately practise it — the only way Tajweed is truly learned.

Understanding the Quran

Do you recite the Quran in prayer without understanding a word?

Deepen your connection with the Quran through our Quranic Arabic and Tafseer courses. Recognise the words you hear in prayer, understand common vocabulary, and unlock a layer of the Quran that transforms how you experience your worship. This is one of the most requested courses we offer.

Reconnect your family with their Islamic roots through our Islamic Studies programme — Aqeedah, Seerah, Hadith, Fiqh, Islamic manners, and daily Adhkar. Practical knowledge taught in a clear, accessible way that builds a genuine Muslim identity from a young age.

About Talqeen Academy

Talqeen Academy is the live-teaching arm of Talqeen — the educational platform behind the Talqeen Quran-reading app. While the app teaches letter-by-letter Quran reading at your own pace, the Academy offers live one-to-one lessons with qualified human teachers for those who want personal guidance, structured courses, and ongoing accountability.

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Whoever takes a path upon which to obtain knowledge, Allah makes the path to Paradise easy for him."

Our teachers are native Arab speakers with expertise in Quranic sciences, Tajweed, Arabic language instruction, and Islamic Studies. We serve students of all ages — children, adults, converts, and complete beginners — across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and beyond.
