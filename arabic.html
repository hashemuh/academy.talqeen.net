Syllabus and Learning Stages

This sequence is adapted to the assessed starting level; familiar material can be reviewed and difficult skills revisited.

1

Choose your pathway

Distinguish conversation, Modern Standard Arabic, Quranic Arabic and children's foundations; agree on the variety and skills to prioritise.

2

Build useful foundations

Develop relevant sounds, vocabulary, letter reading and sentence patterns through clear examples.

3

Use language in context

Practise short conversations, listening tasks, reading passages or guided writing according to the chosen pathway.

4

Extend accuracy and independence

Develop grammar and vocabulary through more demanding tasks, revising the patterns needed to communicate or study confidently.

What Happens in a Lesson

  1. Review vocabulary or a short task from the previous lesson.
  2. Hear a model conversation or read a level-appropriate text.
  3. Practise useful expressions through questions, role-play or sentence building.
  4. Receive feedback on pronunciation, meaning and language form.
  5. Complete a brief reading, listening, speaking or writing task between lessons.

Assessment and Feedback

Assessment reflects the chosen pathway: a conversation task for speaking, a reading sample for comprehension, a short writing task or vocabulary and grammar checks. Success means using the studied language in a suitable task, not simply recognising a rule.

Materials and Setup

  • A teacher-selected textbook or graded material appropriate to the pathway.
  • A vocabulary notebook with examples rather than isolated translations alone.
  • Suitable audio and short reading or writing exercises.
  • A device that supports clear audio; a notebook is useful for letter and writing practice.

Confirm the required edition and any material costs with the team before purchasing resources.

Realistic Progression

Conversation, Quranic Arabic and advanced textual study are different goals. Agree on manageable milestones and review them as skills develop. No single course duration or universal fluency deadline applies to all learners.

Possible next step: Islamic studies with suitable language support. The teacher can help you choose when the foundations are ready.
