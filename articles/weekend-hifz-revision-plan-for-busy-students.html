A Weekend Hifz Revision Plan for Busy Students

Talqeen Academy · Updated 5 October 2026, 00:26:37 (UTC+03:00)

School, university and work can leave very little time on weekdays for deep revision. Weekends give you longer, calmer stretches, which makes them well suited to revising what you have already memorised.

This plan assumes you do a small daily check on weekdays and use the weekend for the heavy lifting.

The idea

Weekday revision stays light: five to ten minutes to keep the recent lessons warm. Weekends do the larger rotation through older material (manzil). The aim is to cycle through everything you have memorised on a predictable schedule so that nothing goes unvisited for too long.

Step 1: Divide what you know into parts

Split your memorised portion into equal parts, for example one part per juz or per five pages. Number them. If you have memorised 3 juz and want to cover them all every four weekends, you can assign about three or four parts per weekend.

Step 2: Sample weekend schedule

WhenTask
Saturday morningWarm up with the recent weekday lessons (15 minutes), then revise the first assigned part aloud
Saturday afternoonSecond assigned part, then note anything that stumbled
Sunday morningThird assigned part, plus targeted repair of the weak spots from Saturday
Sunday eveningSelf-test: recite one random page from memory without looking; check afterwards

Adjust the number of parts to your real time. A smaller rotation that you complete beats a large plan you abandon.

Step 3: Test yourself

  • Ask a family member to open the mushaf at a random page and read the first words; you continue.
  • Recite the last verse of a page and try to reconstruct the first.
  • Record yourself and listen back for hesitations.

Hesitation points are the places to revisit first.

Step 4: Repair weak spots

Keep a list of the verses that you stumbled on. Spend the first minutes of your next revision on them, reading slowly with the text in front of you, then without.

Keep a teacher in the loop

A weekly or fortnightly lesson with a Hifz teacher gives your revision an audience and a corrector. Many students book one lesson at the weekend so that revision and correction happen together. See Hifz at Talqeen.

If you miss a weekend

Do not double up. Resume the rotation where you left off and keep going. If you miss several, a gentler restart is covered in how to restart Hifz after a break.
