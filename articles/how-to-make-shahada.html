How to Make the Shahada: Words, Meaning and First-Month Guidance

Talqeen Academy · Updated 5 October 2026, 08:35:26 (UTC+03:00)

To make the Shahada, state sincerely that no one is worthy of worship except Allah and that Muhammad ﷺ is His Messenger. Understand the testimony and accept it as your own belief. A witness can help explain and support the decision, but a building, paid session or certificate does not create the religious commitment.

The guide below provides the Arabic wording, a word-by-word explanation, practical preparation and a first-month learning plan. It also separates helpful customs from conditions of the testimony, so a newcomer is not burdened with unsupported requirements.

The declaration of faith

Shahada means testimony or witnessing. The word is associated with the Arabic root shahida. In Islam it refers to the two testimonies that affirm exclusive worship of Allah and acceptance of His Messenger's guidance. Bukhari 8 places this testimony among the five foundations of Islam.

أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰه

Transliteration: Ash-hadu an lā ilāha illā Allāh, wa ash-hadu anna Muḥammadan rasūlullāh.

Meaning: I bear witness that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.

The first part rejects worship of anything other than Allah. The second connects belief to the message taught by Muhammad ﷺ. The testimony is not simply a statement of general interest in religion; its meaning needs to be accepted sincerely.

Understanding the conditions without creating barriers

Scholars commonly teach seven conditions associated with the testimony: knowledge, certainty, acceptance, submission, truthfulness, sincerity and love. These describe its inward meaning and implications. A newcomer is not required to memorise a technical checklist before making an understood declaration.

The source called its seven ceremonial steps the seven conditions and presented the formulation as a universal consensus. The conditions of belief and optional practical arrangements are not the same list. Distinguish them before discussing a bath, a location or witnesses.

Essential meaningUnderstand and sincerely accept both testimonies.
LearningUnanswered questions about details are not the same as rejecting the testimony.
FreedomDo not state the words solely to satisfy social pressure while withholding belief.

A practical sequence for making the Shahada

1. Learn about purification

A bath is not a condition that must be completed before the declaration. Scholars differ over ghusl because of conversion, and other reasons for obligatory purification may apply. You can learn and perform the relevant purification afterwards without making it a barrier to sincere belief.

2. Choose a suitable setting

A quiet space may help you listen and speak with understanding. A mosque is not required. The source recommends facing the qiblah, but provides no evidence that this is a special established requirement or recommendation for conversion. Do not turn a practical preference into a ritual condition.

3. Make the decision sincerely

Form your intention in your heart. Bukhari 1 teaches the importance of intention in actions; you do not need an extra spoken formula of intention. Make the decision because you believe the message, not because the setting is emotional or someone insists.

4. State both testimonies

Say the words clearly at a pace you can understand. If Arabic pronunciation is difficult, express their meaning in your language while learning the Arabic. A teacher can model the wording and help you practise without treating minor beginner difficulties as grounds for despair.

The source recommends three repetitions and raising an index finger as an established conversion practice. One sincere declaration is sufficient; the supplied text does not establish a special three-repeat Sunnah or an obligatory gesture for entering Islam. Do not confuse familiar prayer gestures with a conversion requirement.

5. Arrange witnesses if useful

A trusted Muslim, knowledgeable teacher or imam can hear the testimony and help afterwards. If no witness is available, that does not make a sincere private declaration dependent on a future appointment. Witnessing is particularly useful when documentation or community support is needed.

6. Learn prayer

The source presents two voluntary rakahs immediately afterwards as a strongly recommended conversion ritual without establishing a reference. A newcomer should instead prioritise learning the obligatory prayers and purification. Voluntary prayer can be discussed with a teacher, but it is not a condition of becoming Muslim.

7. Make a personal dua

Thank Allah and ask for guidance, forgiveness and steadiness in words you understand. You do not need a special formula or a guaranteed emotional experience. The source's sample request for help living as a Muslim can be used as personal wording, not attributed to the Prophet as a prescribed prayer.

What changes after the testimony?

Sahih Muslim 121 describes Islam wiping away what preceded it. Quran 8:38 also speaks of forgiveness for what came before when disbelief ceases. These teachings offer hope in a fresh beginning.

The source extends forgiveness to every consequence of past actions. That is too broad. Debts, property claims, legal consequences and duties towards people may remain. Ask about those responsibilities rather than assuming religious forgiveness cancels them all.

Forgiveness and responsibilityExplanation
HopeDo not let past sins prevent a sincere new beginning.
Other peopleAddress debts, property and rights carefully.
Future conductContinue repentance and correction when mistakes occur.

The five pillars and the new learner

PillarWhat to learn next
ShahadaUnderstand the declaration you have made.
SalahLearn the five daily prayers and their purification requirements immediately, with suitable guidance while learning.
SawmFast Ramadan when its obligations and conditions apply, including if you embrace Islam during the month.
ZakatLearn which wealth qualifies and the applicable threshold and timing; 2.5% is not a rate for every asset type.
HajjLearn the once-in-a-lifetime obligation when the conditions of ability apply.

The source says fasting begins only at the next Ramadan. Someone who embraces Islam during Ramadan needs guidance for the remainder of the present month; the timing should not be simplified to an automatic year-long delay.

The Quran's revelation over roughly twenty-three years is historical context, not a personal permission to defer obligations for twenty-three years. Learning can be gradual while immediate duties are approached with appropriate instruction and recognised accommodations.

Community participation and practical documentation

A new Muslim belongs to the Muslim community and can seek participation in worship and community life. Marriage, burial, pilgrimage and particular facilities have their own religious and administrative conditions. The source's list should not be read as blanket approval for every marriage or unrestricted entry into every place.

A Shahada certificate records a declaration for a practical purpose; it is not a religious condition. A mosque or organisation may issue one. Some institutions may request proof for marriage, burial or pilgrimage arrangements, but current travel and official requirements need direct confirmation rather than an old article's universal claim.

The source lists employers and institutions as possible users of a certificate. That is context-specific, not a general obligation to disclose faith to every employer. Confirm who needs the document, why and what information is necessary. No guarantee of certificate issuance is transferred to this academy.

Week 2Foundational beliefStudy Allah, angels, revealed books, messengers, the Last Day and divine decree; continue daily prayer learning.
Week 3Supportive communityAsk a local mosque or trusted mentor about beginner help. Attend appropriate gatherings and ask questions without expecting every setting to be equally welcoming.
Week 4Continuing studyArrange a structured course or reliable alternative, and begin reading an accessible Quran translation with context.

The weeks are an organisational example, not religious deadlines. You can learn basic belief earlier and contact support immediately. The source's instruction to start with only two daily prayers should not imply that the others are optional until later.

Previous declarations, doubts and pressure

If you previously made the testimony sincerely and continue to believe, repeating it can be a personal renewal. A history of explicitly abandoning belief is different from drifting in practice; obtain knowledgeable guidance rather than treating every situation as identical.

Uncertainty about an unfamiliar detail does not necessarily negate acceptance of the core testimony. But the source's suggestion that “partial sincerity” under social pressure is enough needs care: a declaration without genuine belief cannot be repaired by a ceremonial setting. Make your own understood commitment freely.

You do not need to give up every enjoyable activity merely because you become Muslim. Learn which practices are allowed, restricted or required, and address specific questions. Gradual improvement should not be turned into a blanket exemption from all duties.

A woman may state the testimony independently. A name change is ordinarily optional, and family announcement can be managed according to circumstances. See reversion and returning to practice for related distinctions.

Related learning and evidence

The source's annual figures of 25,000 Americans and 6,000 British converts, and its population total of 1.8 billion, have no clear dated method in the supplied text and conflict with figures in its companion articles. They should not be promoted as current statistics. Religious conviction is independent of a demographic slogan.
