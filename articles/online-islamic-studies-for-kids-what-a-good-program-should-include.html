Online Islamic Studies for Kids: What a Good Program Should Include

Talqeen Academy · Updated 5 October 2026, 00:26:15 (UTC+03:00)

Islamic Studies for children is about more than facts. A good program helps a child understand their faith, practise it with confidence and develop good character. When comparing programs, it helps to know what to expect.

Here is what a strong online Islamic Studies program for children should include.

Core beliefs, explained simply

Children should learn the basics of belief in language they understand: who Allah is, the Prophets, the Books and the Last Day. Explanations should be clear, age-appropriate and gentle, building love rather than fear.

Worship: how to pray and why

Practical knowledge of wudu, prayer and fasting, learned step by step with demonstration and practice. Children should know both the how and the why.

The Seerah and stories of the Prophets

Stories are how children connect with history and values. A good program tells them accurately, in an age-appropriate way, and links each to a lesson for daily life.

Character and manners (adab)

  • Honesty, kindness, respect for parents and teachers.
  • Good manners in eating, speaking and learning.
  • Everyday supplications (duas) to use at home and at school.

Connection with the Quran

Islamic Studies should connect with Quran learning: short surahs, their meanings and how they relate to what the child is learning. Programs that combine Quran reading with Islamic Studies often reinforce both.

Teaching approach and teachers

  • Qualified teachers with real knowledge and experience with children.
  • Interactive lessons with questions, not only lecturing.
  • Clear progression from one level to the next.
  • Regular feedback to parents.

Questions to ask a program

  • What topics are covered at each age?
  • How are lessons adapted for different ages?
  • How do you assess understanding?
