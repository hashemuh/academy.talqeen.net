Quran Memorization Schedules: Seven Examples with Accurate Targets

Talqeen Academy · Updated 5 October 2026, 08:35:26 (UTC+03:00)

A Quran memorization schedule should make room for new work, recent revision, older passages and correction. The right workload depends on reading accuracy, retained material and available time, rather than the most ambitious title.

The source proposed beginner sessions of fifteen to thirty minutes and advanced periods of one to two hours, alongside more intensive plans. These are examples, not universal prescriptions. Keep rest and actual performance in view.

How to Read These Seven Schedules

All source time slots and page assignments are retained below with rewritten task descriptions. They are timetable examples, not fixed prayer times. Check local Salah times, school or work commitments and sleep before using them.

Named six-, nine-, twelve-, eighteen- and twenty-four-month labels do not necessarily follow from their tables. Calculations below assume a 604-page Mushaf, no prior memorization and all pages needing new learning. Review and final assessment add time.

1. Fast-Paced Daily Example

The source described five to six hours of daily work, but these listed slots total four hours and fifteen minutes. Its heading did not clearly specify a month count. Use the actual workload instead of inventing a completion date.

The explicit fresh assignments total two to three pages, although the source claimed three to four. It also proposed six to eight reviewed pages daily; that figure depends on how repetition and overlapping portions are counted. Record distinct material separately from repeated page visits.

Keep one lighter day weekly, reduce assignments for difficult passages and use accurate listening or secure prayer recitation as reinforcement.

A lighter weekend day, listening practice and smaller chunks for difficult ayahs may make the workload more practical. Silent recall cannot replace hearing and correcting pronunciation.

Use breaks in longer sessions and include listening or secure recitation in Salah. A daily small review can keep contact with the assignment, but it does not guarantee zero forgetting.

Comparing Completion Estimates

The source estimated about two years at one page daily, one year at two pages daily, six to eight months at three to four, and three to four months at five or more. These ranges can include different review allowances; they are not measured predictions.

Simple new-learning arithmetic gives 604 days at one page, 302 at two, roughly 151–202 at three to four, and about 121 at five. Add non-learning days, corrected relearning and final review. Reading speed is not the same as dependable Hifz.

Choosing and Reviewing a Plan

Start with an assessed assignment, track errors and adjust the schedule when recall weakens. The source advertised personalised classes for UK learners, adults, children and ladies. Those are provider claims, not confirmed Talqeen results.
