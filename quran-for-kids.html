Syllabus and Learning Stages

This sequence is adapted to the assessed starting level; familiar material can be reviewed and difficult skills revisited.

1

Readiness and starting level

Observe letter recognition, listening, communication and comfort with a short online activity.

2

Engaging reading foundations

Use sound practice, letter matching and short reading tasks appropriate to the child's level.

3

Short passages and correction

Practise suitable Quranic words or surahs, revisiting pronunciation and explaining simple meanings when appropriate.

4

Routine and gradual independence

Build a manageable review habit and increase the task only when participation, reading and recall support it.

What Happens in a Lesson

  1. Start with a brief familiar review or sound activity.
  2. Introduce one manageable reading or recitation goal.
  3. Alternate oral practice with a suitable matching or listening task.
  4. Give calm, specific correction and another chance to practise.
  5. Agree on a short home activity with the parent or guardian.

Assessment and Feedback

The teacher checks participation and actual reading or recall through small tasks. Parents can discuss what has become easier, which sounds still need work and whether the workload suits the child; speed is not the sole measure of progress.

Materials and Setup

  • A suitable beginner text or Mushaf according to the child's starting level.
  • Teacher-selected age-appropriate reading and listening activities.
  • A simple practice record for parents and the child.
  • A quiet setup with adult help when needed for technology or participation.

Confirm the required edition and any material costs with the team before purchasing resources.

Realistic Progression

Age alone does not determine readiness. Use short sessions and realistic home practice, and adjust the plan if the child becomes tired or the material needs more review. Confirm lesson length, teacher preference and family participation with the team.

Possible next step: Hifz when reading and readiness support memorization. The teacher can help you choose when the foundations are ready.
