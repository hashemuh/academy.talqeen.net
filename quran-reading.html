Syllabus and Learning Stages

This sequence is adapted to the assessed starting level; familiar material can be reviewed and difficult skills revisited.

1

Letters and sounds

Recognise the Arabic letters, distinguish similar shapes and practise their sounds with a clear spoken model.

2

Vowels and word building

Read fatha, kasra and damma; connect letters in different positions and blend short words without guessing.

3

Essential reading marks

Apply sukoon, tanween, shadda and introductory madd through graded word and phrase practice.

4

Guided Quran reading

Read short Quranic passages, practise basic stopping and consolidate corrections before increasing passage length.

What Happens in a Lesson

  1. Revisit a small set of words from the previous lesson.
  2. Listen to a sound or reading pattern demonstrated by the teacher.
  3. Read graded examples aloud and receive specific correction.
  4. Apply the pattern in short words or a suitable Quranic passage.
  5. Agree on a manageable practice task for the next lesson.

Assessment and Feedback

The teacher listens to your reading at the trial and during later checkpoints. Progress is judged through accurate recognition, blending and reading without repeated prompting, rather than the number of pages covered.

Materials and Setup

  • A teacher-selected beginner reading text, such as an appropriate Noor Al-Bayan or Qaida sequence.
  • A clear, consistently used Mushaf for passage practice when you are ready.
  • A notebook or simple error log for letter sounds and reading patterns.
  • A device with a working microphone and a quiet place for oral practice.

Confirm the required edition and any material costs with the team before purchasing resources.

Realistic Progression

Some learners need repeated work on a small group of sounds; others can move more quickly through familiar material. The next stage begins when you can apply the skill reliably. Lesson frequency and home practice influence progress, so no universal completion date is promised.

Possible next step: Tajweed and recitation practice. The teacher can help you choose when the foundations are ready.
