Our Story

Talqeen Academy exists because the Quran deserves to be taught by a human teacher — patiently, correctly, and with the heart. We are the live-teaching platform of Talqeen, the educational brand behind the Talqeen Quran-reading app.

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "The best of you are those who learn the Quran and teach it." — Sahih Al-Bukhari

Our teachers are qualified native Arab speakers — many with Ijazah certifications — who have dedicated their lives to Quran education. We serve Muslim families across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and beyond, providing live one-to-one lessons that fit into modern life.

We believe that technology and human teaching are not in competition. The Talqeen app builds your independent reading foundation. Talqeen Academy adds the teacher who corrects your recitation, answers your questions, and keeps you accountable.

Our Teachers

Every teacher at Talqeen Academy is a qualified native Arab speaker. We do not hire untrained tutors.

Ijazah Holders

Many of our Quran teachers hold an Ijazah — a formal certificate of recitation transmitted through an unbroken chain to the Prophet ﷺ himself.

University Qualified

Our Arabic and Islamic Studies teachers hold formal university qualifications in Arabic language, Islamic sciences, and education.

Experienced Educators

Every teacher has significant classroom experience — not just knowledge, but the skill to communicate it to students of every age and background.

The Talqeen Brand

Talqeen Academy and the Talqeen app share the same mission: making Quran education accessible, effective, and beautiful for every Muslim, wherever they are in the world.

The Talqeen App

A self-paced Quran reading app that teaches Arabic letters from zero using the Noor Al-Bayan curriculum — with on-device speech recognition, games, and a full 23-stage programme. The perfect complement to live lessons at Talqeen Academy.
