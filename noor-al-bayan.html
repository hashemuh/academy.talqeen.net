What is Noor Al-Bayan?

Noor Al-Bayan (نور البيان) is the most widely used Quran reading curriculum in Islamic schools across the world. It teaches reading through a harakah-first, phonics-based method — building each sound systematically before combining letters into words.

Harakah-First Method

All 28 letters are taught with Fatha first — then Kasra, then Damma — before moving to joined letters and full words. Every stage reinforces the one before.

Structured 23-Stage Curriculum

From simple vowelled letters through Madd, Sukoon, Tanween, and Shadda — right up to connected speech rules and advanced Tajweed marks.

Designed for All Ages

Originally developed for children, Noor Al-Bayan is equally effective for adults and converts coming to Quran reading for the first time.

Mastery-Based Progress

Students do not advance until they have genuinely mastered each stage. There is no rushing — only solid, durable learning.

Syllabus and Learning Stages

This sequence is adapted to the assessed starting level; familiar material can be reviewed and difficult skills revisited.

1

Letters and short vowels

Recognise letters and practise fatha, kasra and damma through clear oral models.

2

Joining and blending

Read letters in connected forms and build short words without relying on memorised shapes.

3

Reading marks and lengths

Practise sukoon, tanween, shadda and introductory madd in suitable graded examples.

4

Connected passage reading

Apply the foundations to short Quranic words and passages while consolidating accuracy.

What Happens in a Lesson

  1. Revisit sounds or patterns needing another check.
  2. Hear the teacher model a new reading pattern.
  3. Read graded examples aloud with correction.
  4. Apply the pattern in connected words.
  5. Practise a small assigned set before the next lesson.

Assessment and Feedback

The teacher checks accurate recognition and independent blending before adding more complex examples. Moving through pages is not the only measure: you should be able to read unfamiliar examples using the studied pattern.

Materials and Setup

  • A teacher-selected Noor Al-Bayan text or suitable phonics exercises.
  • A notebook for difficult sounds and word patterns.
  • A clear Mushaf for guided passage reading when appropriate.
  • Optional Talqeen app practice alongside, rather than instead of, live feedback.

Confirm the required edition and any material costs with the team before purchasing resources.

Realistic Progression

The sequence is adapted to the learner's starting skills and pace. Some patterns need several lessons; others are already familiar. Agree on practice and review rather than expecting completion within a fixed number of weeks.

Possible next step: Guided Quran reading after phonics foundations. The teacher can help you choose when the foundations are ready.
