What is Noor Al-Bayan?

Noor Al-Bayan (نور البيان) is the most widely used Quran reading curriculum in Islamic schools across the world. It teaches reading through a harakah-first, phonics-based method — building each sound systematically before combining letters into words.

Harakah-First Method

All 28 letters are taught with Fatha first — then Kasra, then Damma — before moving to joined letters and full words. Every stage reinforces the one before.

Structured 23-Stage Curriculum

From simple vowelled letters through Madd, Sukoon, Tanween, and Shadda — right up to connected speech rules and advanced Tajweed marks.

Designed for All Ages

Originally developed for children, Noor Al-Bayan is equally effective for adults and converts coming to Quran reading for the first time.

Mastery-Based Progress

Students do not advance until they have genuinely mastered each stage. There is no rushing — only solid, durable learning.
